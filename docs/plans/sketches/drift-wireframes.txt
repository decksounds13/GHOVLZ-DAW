  body when the device type is drift. Six generic knobs are not enough:
  the transfer pair and the two harmonic rows are the point of the device.

  Tape mode adds a second row, hidden for the other modes:
  solver, speed, bias, gap, spacing, thickness, wow, flutter, chew, degrade.
  Those controls stay off the lane.

TRACK TOGGLE (later, not the first ship)

  mixer strip, under the insert slots:

  +--------+
  | ANALOG |  <- gold when on, line color when off
  | o tiny |  <- one trim, opens nothing
  +--------+

  It is a console-mode preset of the same section, with tolerance, hiss,
  and crosstalk. It does not draw the transfer pair. The core stereo-motion
  test is a Drift acceptance test, not a mixer-strip test.
